Foundation Skills

Little Voices, Big Words

Help young learners expand their vocabulary, express their big ideas with confidence, and build early storytelling and conversational skills in a fun, engaging way.

Learning Objectives

  • Introduce fun, descriptive vocabulary words to replace simple everyday terms.
  • Encourage kids to express their thoughts, feelings, and questions clearly.
  • Build confidence in sharing stories, ideas, and opinions during group activities.

Key Kid-Friendly "Big Words"

Exciting new words that kids can use to describe their day, feelings, and imagination.

"I am feeling completely exhausted after playtime!"

"That puzzle was quite challenging, but I figured it out!"

"Let's collaborate to build an even bigger block tower."

Speaking Practice

Engage children in playful role-play and show-and-tell sessions to practice using their new vocabulary out loud.

💡 Teacher/Parent Tip:

Praise children whenever they use a "big word" in context and gently help them rephrase simple sentences into more descriptive ones.

Listening Practice

Listen to interactive stories and audio prompts to help children spot new words and understand how characters express their emotions.

💡 Teacher/Parent Tip:

Pause during storytelling to ask kids what a specific descriptive word means or how the story's character might be feeling.
