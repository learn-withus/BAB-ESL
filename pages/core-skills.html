Core Skills

The Art of Active Listening

Master the art of listening, understanding, and responding with purpose to build deeper connections, prevent misunderstandings, and elevate your interpersonal communication.

Learning Objectives

  • Move beyond hearing to fully comprehend verbal and non-verbal cues.
  • Practice reflective listening and purposeful validation techniques.
  • Formulate thoughtful, constructive responses that keep conversations meaningful.

Key Listening & Response Expressions

Frameworks for confirming understanding, clarifying intent, and responding thoughtfully.

"What I'm hearing is that you felt..."

"Could you elaborate a bit more on how that impacted..."

"That makes complete sense given your perspective on..."

Speaking Practice

Practice summarizing complex points, asking open-ended clarifying questions, and responding with empathy and precision.

💡 Educator Tip:

Encourage learners to repeat back or paraphrase what their partner said before sharing their own counter-argument or opinion.

Listening Practice

Deconstruct audio scenarios to catch underlying emotional tones, unstated assumptions, and core conversational takeaways.

💡 Educator Tip:

Have students focus on identifying the speaker's primary intent rather than just listening for surface-level vocabulary words.
